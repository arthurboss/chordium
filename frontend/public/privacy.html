Chordium Privacy Policy

Effective 21 November 2026

Chordium is a guitar chord sheet viewer. It is developed by Arthur Boss. This policy describes what the app does with data on your device and what leaves it.

Data kept on your device

Saved chord sheets, preferences, and import or export files stay in the app's local storage. They are not uploaded to a Chordium account. You can remove them by deleting songs in the app or clearing the app's storage in Android settings.

Camera and microphone

The camera is used to scan QR codes for jam sessions. The microphone is used for the tuner and, where supported, voice search. Camera frames and audio are processed on the device. Chordium does not upload them to its servers. When voice search uses the device's speech service, that service may process audio under its own privacy policy.

Search

Search terms are sent to the Chordium API so it can find artists and songs. The API does not create a user account or store a search history tied to you. It may keep public artist and song metadata returned by searches. Hosting and database providers may keep short-lived technical request logs.

Backups

If Android backup is enabled on your device, Android may copy app data to your Google account so it can be restored after reinstalling the app. That backup is controlled by Android and your Google account settings.

Sharing

When you share a song or jam link, the link is created on your device and passed to the app you choose. Chordium does not receive a copy of what you share.

Third parties

Chordium uses Vercel for hosting and serverless functions and Neon for its song and artist database. These providers process requests on Chordium's behalf. Chordium does not sell personal data and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Children

Chordium is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Privacy questions can be raised at github.com/arthurboss/chordium/issues.
